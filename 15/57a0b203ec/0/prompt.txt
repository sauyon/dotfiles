## Nits

1. **`orca.nix` (installCheckPhase, `| sha256sum -c --quiet`):** there's no `--strict`.
   - Without it, `sha256sum -c` warns about badly formatted lines and skips them; it only fails when *every* line is bad.
   - Failure scenario: upstream changes some pins to SRI `sha256-…` strings or to objects. `"\(.value)"` then prints lines `sha256sum` can't parse, which are silently skipped, and the build passes with those pins never checked.

2. **`orca.nix` (installCheckPhase, `lsx` inside `diff &lt;(…) &lt;(…)`):** failures inside a process substitution don't trip `set -e` or `pipefail`.
