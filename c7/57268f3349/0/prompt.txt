Backtick is the same class. Reachability is essentially nil (the admin's `$HOME` would have to contain `$` or a backtick), so it stays a nit — but the comment "it is interpolated now (it must be, to survive the variable going out of scope)" is not true: a bash EXIT trap runs in the same shell, and `$rd` is still in scope. `trap cleanup EXIT` with a `cleanup()` that reads `$work`/`$rd` directly needs no interpolation and no quoting hazard at all.
