  ### I1. `$count` is dereferenced unguarded in both message branches, defeating the `${count:-1}` defence (home.nix:2527, 2529, 2538)
  
  The guard is careful (`[ "''${count:-1}" -ne 0 ]`), and then both bodies immediately do the unguarded thing:


  - **keybind:** exits 1 *before* `notify-send` runs. The notification is the entire reason the branch exists ("stderr from a keybind lands in the Hyprland log, where nobody looks") and it is exactly the case where it doesn't fire. Silent.

The only realistic trigger is `seq` missing from PATH, and this script takes `hyprctl`, `seq`, `sleep` and `notify-send` from the ambient PATH — in the systemd ExecStop context that's the user manager's environment, not a store-pinned PATH. Low probability, but it's precisely the environment the `:-1` was written to survive.
