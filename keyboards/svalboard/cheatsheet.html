Reading the diagram

Each finger sits in a cup of five switches: North, South, East, West and Center (the plain down-press). East is screen-right on both hands.

Dashed = dead key. Grey italic glyphs on layers 1 and 2 fall through to layer 0.

Layer 15 — mouse

No key reaches it. The firmware switches over when the pointer moves and leaves on the first key that isn't on the layer.

Buttons 1/3/2 on index/middle/ring South, both hands. Pinky Center recalibrates the pointer.

Departures from the port

g is on the left index's West so the index keeps two of its three inner-column letters; v is exiled to the middle finger.

- : ( ) & @ ~ are promoted to layer 0 and are holes on layer 1, never on two keys.
